Experimental v0.2 prototype

Compare an idealized flat petal against a parametric surface. The same surface equations generate rose-like and tulip-like whorls.

Scientific status: Curvature is a discrete normal-variation proxy, not a calibrated Gaussian or mean curvature measurement. Growth is interpolated geometry, not a biological differential-growth solver. Correlated variation is deterministic and illustrative. The baseline is a deliberately simplified comparison, not a reconstruction of the current production flower mesh. No collision or self-intersection guarantee is provided. Performance numbers are device-specific and exclude shader compilation.
